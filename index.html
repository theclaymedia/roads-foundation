The ROADS Difference

Whole-Person Healthcare Under One Roof

We break down traditional healthcare silos. At ROADS, your medical doctor, therapist, dentist, and social service case manager work as a unified team to support your overall wellness.

1. Primary Medical Care

Comprehensive adult medicine, pediatrics, chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension), immunizations, physicals, and preventative health screenings.

  • Board-certified doctors & nurses
  • Same-day appointments available

2. Behavioral Health & Psychiatry

Individual and group therapy, psychiatric evaluation, medication management, trauma-informed care, and specialized substance use disorder programs (Matrix Model).

  • Licensed therapists & psychiatrists
  • Culturally sensitive, bilingual care

3. Dental & Social Support

Cleanings, extractions, crowns, and oral surgery, paired with active case management, housing navigation, food security support, and Medi-Cal enrollment assistance.

  • Modern dental suites
  • Dedicated patient navigation team

Healthcare is a Human Right. We Turn No One Away.

Whether you have full coverage, partial coverage, or no insurance at all, ROADS Community Clinics provides comprehensive medical, behavioral, and dental care.

Sliding Fee Discount Scale

Fees are discounted based on your household income and family size following Federal Poverty Guidelines. Proof of income (paystub, tax return, or self-declaration statement) is all you need to qualify.
